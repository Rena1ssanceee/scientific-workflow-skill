# Literature List Template

The master list should retain all eligible and plausibly eligible records within the frozen scope. A separate priority field may identify the core-reading subset, but low-priority records must not be silently omitted from the master list.

Recommended columns:

1. Record ID
2. Title
3. First author
4. Year
5. Journal or source
6. DOI
7. PMID or other identifier
8. Source database or discovery route
9. Search query family or citation route
10. Research object or disease
11. Study design
12. Data type
13. Sample size and analytical unit
14. Main method
15. Public data accession
16. Relevance to current project
17. Priority: P0/P1/P2
18. Full-text status
19. Inclusion status
20. Duplicate/version relationship
21. Notes

Priority convention:

- P0: directly determines the final plan or methods.
- P1: important supporting evidence or adjacent design.
- P2: background, broad review, lower direct relevance, or a record retained for completeness.

Also keep a separate search log containing database, date, exact query, filters, retrieved count, deduplicated count, screened count, and coverage notes.
